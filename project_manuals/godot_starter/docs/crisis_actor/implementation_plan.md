# CRISIS ACTOR: The Rehearsal State — P0 Refinement Plan

This plan details the refinements to be applied to the rulebook, character sheets, and Episode 1 scenario to resolve contradictions, balance PC abilities, and focus the scenario on information-based tension for the P0 test.

## Proposed Changes

### 1. [MODIFY] [crisis_actor_rulebook.md](crisis_actor_rulebook.md)
- **Ability Names**: Standardize all abilities to **"脚本 (Scenario)" / "演出 (Staging)" / "情報 (Information)" / "精神 (Psyche)"**.
- **Black Card Rule**: Explicitly define that Black Cards cannot be destroyed, but can be "neutralized" by creating corresponding White Cards that deny or discredit them.

### 2. [MODIFY] [crisis_actor_characters.md](crisis_actor_characters.md)
- **Ability Scores**: Match the standardized 4-ability system for all PCs.
- **PC2 (Auditor)**: Fix Clara's backstory (Clara is alive; her sister Erina died). Clarify victory condition based on preventing Black Card neutralization.
- **PC3 (Engineer)**: Add **"機材劣化クロック (Equipment Decay Clock: 0~3)"**. Using special skills increases this; at 3, it triggers Reality Intrusion or immediate Gray Card decay.
- **PC4 (Manager)**: Shift victory condition from general intrusion to a specific **"保護対象 (Protected Target)"** card, linking their survival to PC4's victory.

### 3. [MODIFY] [crisis_actor_scenario_ep1.md](crisis_actor_scenario_ep1.md)
- **Clara / Kramer Settings**: Resolve the Clara contradiction (Clara alive, Erina dead). Define Kramer as a self-preserving bureaucrat who only knows the "underground ban".
- **Incident Tweaks**: Replace the "Flooding/Water" threat with **"映像の空白 (Blank Video Footage)"** and **"誘導灯停止 (Exit Signs Out)"** to align with the information-control theme.
- **Yellow Gas as Info-Cards**: Define specific Gray, White, and Black cards for the yellow gas.
- **Climax Options**: Expand the Climax choices into a 3-way decision (Complete Cover-up, Abandon Simulation, Controlled Disclosure).
- **White Card Template**: Fix the exact structure/template for the three final White Cards.
